Player Cards
Every value on the card is a field EA's Pro Clubs API actually returns — no invented pace or dribbling attributes. Tier colour is derived from proOverall, not assigned by hand.

01
OVR block, skewed 12° Reads proOverall. Its fill is the tier colour, so the card's identity is legible at thumbnail size before any text is.
02
Position watermark favoritePosition set oversized at 5.5% white. Gives every card a distinct silhouette without needing a player photo — which Pro Clubs has no source for.
03
Personal form, last 5 That player's own W/D/L from the matches they actually appeared in — not the club's. Comes from the per-match rosters already loaded on the Matches tab.
04
Name in a lower third Pro name over gamertag, in the same broadcast bar used for goal events elsewhere on the site. One motif, reused.
05
Six stats, all real ratingAve, winRate, manOfTheMatch, passSuccessRate, shotSuccessRate, tackleSuccessRate. Goalkeepers swap Shot and Tackle for clean sheets and saves.
06
Season / Career toggle The whole card flips to career* values. Career is the one that survives an FC 27 season reset — worth having on the card the day the new title lands.
02 — Tiers & Variants Colour is a function of OVR, so it updates itself. No manual promotion.
