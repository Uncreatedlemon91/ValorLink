From joining the Discord server to settled in the squad. Staff only.

{# The process, said once, so every coach runs it the same way. #}
  1. Joins the serverNew members show up below. Start a file, or dismiss anyone who isn't here to play.
  2. ProspectAdd their positions and gamertag, and invite them to a training or match.
  3. On trialWrite a note after every session they play{% if feedback_enabled %} — each one is posted to the recruitment channel on Discord{% endif %}.
  4. OfferManagement sends the contract from their file. They accept in Discord.
  5. SignedConfirm the signing: the contract starts and it's announced.
  6. Settling inWork through the checklist on their file: gamertag, EA club invite, welcome, usual nights, a first goal.
